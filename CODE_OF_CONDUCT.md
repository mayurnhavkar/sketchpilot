# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and give clear, specific feedback.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private information.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
